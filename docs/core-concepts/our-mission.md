# Our Mission

OfficeX is a peer-to-peer digital workspace. Our mission is to provide a safe space where freedom works.&#x20;

Chat, Apps & Storage are our core use cases, with anonymous offline standards enabling zero dependency "apocalypse proof" workspaces.&#x20;

Cryptography powers collaboration & privacy, using decentralized trustless protocols to enable unstoppable teamwork.

We combine all these features into a powerful open source SDK, so that any app or agent can offer the same guarantees of convenience & sovereignty as defaults.

### Why users love OfficeX

* Privacy Now - zero spying default confidential
* Free Forever - zero cost unlimited usage

### Why developers love OfficeX

* 100% whitelabel - brand it however you want for your users
* 100% free at scale - public cloud without billing liability
